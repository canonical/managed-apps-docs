Azure
=====

This section covers general operational topics for Canonical Managed Apps on
Azure, applicable across all applications.

Overview
--------

Canonical Managed Apps on Azure provides fully managed, production-grade
open-source applications deployed on Microsoft Azure infrastructure. Our
engineers handle the complete lifecycle — from initial deployment and
configuration through day-to-day operations, upgrades, and security patching.

Supported regions
-----------------

Managed Apps can be deployed in any Azure region where the required services are
available. Contact us to confirm availability for your preferred region and
workload requirements.

Shared responsibility model
---------------------------

Under Canonical Managed Services, responsibilities are divided as follows:

**Canonical manages:**

- Application deployment and configuration
- Monitoring and alerting
- Incident response and troubleshooting
- Security patching and upgrades
- Backup configuration and validation
- Capacity planning recommendations

**Customer manages:**

- Azure subscription and billing
- Network connectivity and firewall rules
- Data ownership and access control
- Business continuity requirements and RTO/RPO definitions

Onboarding process
------------------

1. **Scoping** — Define workload requirements, SLAs, and architecture together
   with Canonical engineers.
2. **Provisioning** — Infrastructure and applications are deployed according to
   the agreed design.
3. **Handover** — Application endpoints, credentials, and operational runbooks
   are delivered to your team.
4. **Steady state** — Canonical assumes ongoing operational responsibility under
   the agreed SLA.

Support and escalation
----------------------

All managed environments include 24/7 monitoring and incident management.
Escalation paths and response times are defined in your service agreement.

For urgent issues, contact your assigned service delivery manager directly or
use the shared communication channel established during onboarding.

`Get in touch <https://ubuntu.com/managed/contact-us>`_
